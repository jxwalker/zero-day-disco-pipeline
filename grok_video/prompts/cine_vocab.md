# Cine vocab for Grok Imagine (19 Oct 2026)
Distilled (own words) from Melies cinematic-techniques, Eyecannndy, AI Camera Movements and GrokFilm; references from
Film Grab / Shot Cafe. Rule of use: per shot pick ONE shot type + ONE lens + ONE light + ONE move. Write the phrase, then
the start frame and the end frame. Grok Imagine follows one plain physical move well and two moves badly.
Never write: cinematic, 4k, 8k, ultra-detailed, masterpiece, beautiful, stunning, epic, hyperreal, award-winning.

## Shot types (size / angle)  `id: prompt phrase`
- ECU: extreme close-up on one detail (an eye, fingertips, a coffee ring), the detail fills the frame
- CU: close-up, face from chin to hairline, background soft and dark
- MCU: medium close-up from mid-chest up, shoulders in frame, eyes on the upper third
- MS: medium shot from the waist up, hands visible and doing something
- COWBOY: mid-thigh-up framing, the person and the object they hold both readable
- FULL: full-length figure head to toe with a little floor and headroom
- WIDE: wide shot, the person small in a readable real place, lots of negative space
- EST: establishing wide of the location before the person enters, quiet and still
- OTS: over-the-shoulder, soft shoulder edge in the foreground, the subject sharp behind it
- INSERT: insert of a hand and an object in action, tabletop height
- TWO: two-shot, both people in one frame, staged in depth not side by side
- LOW: low angle from hip height looking slightly up, ceiling or sky behind
- HIGH: high angle looking down at the person, the floor pattern framing them
- PROFILE: clean side profile against a plain wall, nose pointing into the empty side
- FRAMEINFRAME: subject seen through a doorway, window or hatch, the frame edges dark and soft
- DIRTY: dirty frame, an out-of-focus foreground object covering one edge of the frame
- CENTRAL: symmetrical central framing, subject dead centre facing camera, deadpan

## Lenses / optics
- L24: 24mm wide at f/4, room geography visible, slight perspective stretch at the edges
- L35: 35mm at f/2.8, natural documentary field of view, person plus surroundings
- L50: 50mm at f/2, eye-like perspective, gently soft background
- L85: 85mm portrait lens at f/1.8, face crisp, background melts into soft blur
- L135: 135mm telephoto at f/2.8, compressed background, the person isolated across a distance
- ANA: anamorphic-style 40mm, oval bokeh, faint horizontal flare from a practical light
- SHALLOW: very shallow focus, only the eyes sharp
- RACK: rack focus from the foreground object to the face behind it (counts as the shot's move)

## Lighting setups
- WINDOW: soft side window light from frame left, gentle falloff into shadow on the far cheek
- OVERCAST: flat overcast daylight, soft shadows, muted colour
- GOLDEN: low golden-hour sun from behind, warm rim on hair and shoulders, slight haze
- BLUE: blue-hour dusk, cool ambient sky with one warm practical lamp in frame
- PRACTICAL: lit only by a practical lamp in frame, warm pool of light, dark corners
- TUNGSTEN: warm tungsten interior at night, orange-amber skin, deep brown shadows
- SODIUM: sodium-vapour street light from above, amber cast, inky shadows
- FLUOR: overhead office fluorescent, slightly green-white, flat and banal
- REMBRANDT: Rembrandt key from 45 degrees high, a small triangle of light on the shadow cheek
- RIM: backlight rim separating the figure from a dark background
- SILHOUETTE: person in silhouette against a bright window or sky, no fill
- LOWKEY: low-key, one soft source, most of the frame in shadow
- SCREENGLOW: face lit only by a laptop screen seen from behind, cool glow, room dark
- DAPPLED: dappled sunlight through leaves moving softly across the face
- HAZE: light haze in the air so the window light shows as soft visible beams
- STAGE: small-venue stage light, one warm spot from above and behind, dark crowd shapes

## Camera moves (exactly one per shot)  `id: phrase (start -> end)`
- STATIC: locked-off static camera, nothing moves but the person
- PUSH: slow dolly in toward the subject on a track, real parallax, no zoom; wide-ish start, tighter end
- PULL: slow dolly out away from the subject, more of the room revealed at the end
- TRUCKL / TRUCKR: lateral truck left/right on a track, the lens keeps facing forward, scene slides past
- SLIDER: short slider drift sideways, foreground layer shifting against the subject (small, elegant)
- PANL / PANR: smooth pan left/right from a fixed tripod, horizon level, settle on the new subject
- TILTUP / TILTDN: tilt up/down from a fixed point, from the feet/object to the face (or reverse)
- ARC: shallow arc around the subject to the left/right, distance constant, ends on a new angle
- ORBIT: slow half orbit around the seated subject, background rotating behind them
- TRACK: side tracking shot moving parallel with the walking subject at the same pace
- LEAD: reverse tracking, the camera backs away in front of the walking subject, face stays framed
- FOLLOW: follow from behind at shoulder height as the subject walks into the space
- HANDHELD: gentle handheld at eye height, slight natural sway, no shake
- CRANEUP: slow crane up and back from the subject to reveal the whole location
- PUSHPAST: push past a foreground doorframe or shoulder to arrive on the subject
- SZOOM: very slow zoom in from a locked tripod (no parallax), for deadpan reaction beats
- CRASH: quick crash zoom onto the face (chorus/bridge beats only, max 2 per film)
- WHIP: whip pan to the next subject (chorus only, max 2 per film; never with the lead's face mid-whip)
- DOLLYZOOM: dolly zoom, the background stretches while the face stays the same size (bridge surprise only, max 1)

## Reference archetypes (grade + framing)  -- use the ARCH id in each shot's `ref`
- ARCH-LLEWYN  Inside Llewyn Davis (2013): desaturated winter grey-green and cold teal, beige skin; tight singles, tired
  faces in doorways, overcast window light. Folk / acoustic / sad-sack verses.
- ARCH-PATERSON  Paterson (2016): soft daylight, muted brick red, cream and bus-blue; symmetrical routine framings,
  static wides of small-town repetition. Folk, deadpan routine gags.
- ARCH-FISHTANK  Fish Tank (2009): close handheld, overcast council-estate greys with a sharp sunlit gold; close and
  following behind the lead. Punk, indie rock grit.
- ARCH-AMHONEY  American Honey (2016): golden-hour handheld, sun flare, dusty pink and highway gold; youthful
  motion, faces in car windows. Indie pop, road energy.
- ARCH-AFTERSUN  Aftersun (2022): 35mm warm pastel aqua and peach, soft halation on highlights; long held frames,
  reflections, people at the edge of frame. Bittersweet indie, bridge.
- ARCH-FLORIDA  The Florida Project (2017): saturated candy lilac, mint and sky blue in hard sun; wide static
  frames with small figures, low child-height angles. Pop, absurd brightness.
- ARCH-CONTROL  Control (2007): near-monochrome cold grey and bone, washed-out northern streets; static wides, profiles, deep
  shadow. Post-punk; use as a desaturated "cold" grade.
- ARCH-ENGLAND  This Is England (2006): 16mm-feel grain, olive, oxblood and grey; handheld group two-shots,
  terraced streets. Punk, ska, two-tone.
- ARCH-CHUNGKING  Chungking Express (1994): step-printed night, sodium amber against green fluorescent; tight
  telephoto through glass. Night verses, restless pop.
- ARCH-PARISTEXAS  Paris, Texas (1984): sodium red and neon-green dusk against deep blue sky; lone figure in wide
  landscape, centred frames. Americana, slide-guitar, outro.
- ARCH-LADYBIRD  Lady Bird (2017): soft overcast Californian pastels, faded pink and sage; medium two-shots,
  comic timing in static frames. Indie pop, mother-daughter style banter.
- ARCH-HER  Her (2013): warm coral, red and peach wardrobe against pale haze; shallow focus singles, lonely
  figure in a big clean window. Synth-pop, soft tech satire (screens never legible).
- ARCH-PUNCHDRUNK  Punch-Drunk Love (2002): primary blue suit against fluorescent supermarket white, anamorphic
  flare; long lateral tracks, figure tiny in corridors. Pop-rock, absurd panic.
- ARCH-FRANCESHA  Frances Ha (2012): bright silvery daylight, washed greys and soft whites (the film is B&W; keep colour, just restrained); running tracking shots, deadpan central
  framing. Upbeat indie, chorus energy.
- ARCH-TRAINSPOT  Trainspotting (1996): hard saturated orange, toilet green and grey-blue; wide-angle low angles,
  crash zooms, direct-to-camera. Britpop, punk, bridge chaos.
- ARCH-DRIVE  Drive (2011): sodium orange night, teal shadow, hot pink accent; slow push-ins, static profiles in
  cars, silhouette. Synth / darkwave; use sparingly (neon soup risk).
- ARCH-PARTY  24 Hour Party People (2002): DV-ish handheld, club tungsten and green spill, documentary asides to
  camera. Indie-dance, Madchester, chorus.

Shot Cafe collections worth borrowing as framing ideas: "Frame in a Frame", "Looking Back (rear views)",
"Kitchen Scenes", "Party Vibe"; and its colour filter (palette by hex) for picking a 2-3 colour section palette.

## Template for one shot
`[ARCH-x] [SHOT] [LENS] [LIGHT] [MOVE]` -> e.g. ARCH-PATERSON, CENTRAL, L35, OVERCAST, PUSH:
"Centred symmetrical medium shot, 35mm f/2.8, flat overcast daylight, muted brick red and cream. Slow dolly in on a
track toward her face, real parallax, no zoom; starts waist-up, ends chest-up as she blinks."
