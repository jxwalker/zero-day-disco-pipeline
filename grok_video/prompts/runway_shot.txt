RUNWAY PREMIUM SHOT SHEET (24 Oct 2026; the owner: "runway needs very very good prompting", "be much more of a movie
producer... give exact descriptions"). Based on Runway's own guides (help.runwayml.com Gen-4 Video Prompting Guide,
Image to Video Prompting Guide, Gen-4 Image Prompting Guide; runway.com photo-to-video tips):
 - the START FRAME carries composition, subject, wardrobe, light and style; describe them exhaustively there;
 - the VIDEO prompt describes ONLY motion: camera move first, then subject action, then scene/atmosphere motion;
 - one clear action per 4-5 s clip, one camera move; say "locked camera" if there is no move, or the model drifts;
 - refer to people as "the woman"/"she"/"the man on the left", never re-describe them in the motion prompt;
 - positive, direct language (say what happens, not what should not); negatives go in their own list.

You are the director and line producer. Write ONE shot sheet as JSON for the shot below. EXACT, MEASURABLE, NO VAGUE WORDS.
Banned (the reviewer rejects the sheet if any appears without a number or concrete noun attached): cool, cinematic,
nice, beautiful, stunning, epic, dynamic, vibrant, moody, aesthetic, interesting, dramatic, professional, high quality,
amazing, sleek, modern, gorgeous, vibe, energetic, natural-looking, some, various, etc.

Fields (every one required, every one concrete):
 set: room type, dimensions in metres (W x D x H), wall/floor materials, and 3-6 props each with colour and position
      (frame left/centre/right, foreground/midground/background, distance from camera in m).
 character: age (21-28), gender, ethnicity-neutral physical description, hair (length, colour, style), each wardrobe
      item with exact colour (hex or named + hex), accessories, and expression (deadpan: neutral mouth, eyes level;
      never tongue out, pouting, duck face, gurning or a pulled face).
 blocking: a list of timed beats covering 0.0 s to the clip duration, e.g. "0.0-1.5 s: she lifts her left wrist 15 cm
      to chest height". Every beat names body part, direction, distance or angle.
 camera: body/format, lens in mm and aperture, camera height in m, distance to subject in m, the single move (locked,
      dolly in/out, truck, pedestal, pan), its total travel (cm or degrees) and speed (cm/s or deg/s), start framing and
      end framing (e.g. "start: full body, head 10% from top; end: knees up, head 8% from top"). Push-ins max 15% scale.
 composition: centred, symmetrical wide (Acid Deadpan) unless the shot says otherwise; where the horizon/eyeline sits.
 lighting: key, fill, practicals: each with fixture type, direction (clock position or degrees), height, colour
      temperature in K or gel, and ratio (e.g. key:fill 4:1).
 palette: exact hex values. House: Klein blue #002FA7, acid green #B0FF00, safety orange #FF6700, off-white #FAFAF4;
      chorus drops (Glitch Tape) add magenta #FF00A0 and cyan #00E5FF.
 texture: film stock or texture (e.g. Kodak Vision3 500T 5219, 35 mm, grain level), shutter (180 deg), frame rate.
 start_state / end_state: what the frame shows at 0 s and at the last frame.
 constant: what must not change (face, hair, every wardrobe item, prop positions, palette, light direction).
 negatives: list, at least: no text, no letters, no mirrored or upside-down text, no logos, no tongue out, no pulled
      faces, no extra limbs or fingers, no morphing, no new people, no laptops.
 frame_prompt: the nano-banana start-frame prompt, <= 1800 chars, written from set+character+camera start framing+
      composition+lighting+palette+texture (stills prompt: describe everything visible).
 motion_prompt: the image-to-video prompt, <= 900 chars, motion only, in Runway order: camera move with speed and
      travel; then the timed subject beats; then scene motion; ending with "Faces, wardrobe and props stay unchanged."

25 OCT QC RULES (the owner: "wrenches on her shirt", "floppy wrench in her pocket"):
- Wardrobe = plain, solid-coloured garments only. NO prints, patches, embroidery, logos, badges or graphics, never tool- or prop-shaped motifs.
- NO prop-in-pocket, clip-onto-belt/tool-loop, holster, hang-up or bite-the-prop actions. If the line needs one, show the prop already in place (locked camera) or use a graphic card.
- Rigid props (spanners, tools, watches) stay still or move only with a simple held gesture; end the motion prompt with "Rigid props keep their exact shape and size."
